History

    Practice tool. Calculator rules differ by exam board and school, so use your approved Casio in the exam hall. Press Menu, then 2 for Equation, to solve simultaneous equations and polynomials on the display. Keyboard works too (m opens the menu): digits, + − * / ^ ( ), Enter, Backspace, arrow keys, s c t for sin cos tan, r for √, p for π.
